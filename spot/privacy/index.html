Spot Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Spot (“the app”) is a workout tracker for friends who train together. It is made by Zachary Artman (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, a display name, a handle, and a profile colour. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email that the provider shares with us. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Workout data. The app stores the programs you build, the workouts you log (exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, and timestamps), per-exercise notes, and personal records derived from that data.

Friends. When you add a friend by handle, we store the friendship and its status so that the two of you can see each other's workouts.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data from Apple Health, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data in sync between your devices.
  • To show accepted friends what you are training right now and what you trained recently, and to show you the same about them.
  • To suggest weights, rest times, and equipment based on your own history.
  • To run rest timers as Live Activities and local notifications on your device.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the friends you have accepted.

Who can see your data

Only you and friends you have accepted can see your profile and workouts. Database rules enforce this on the server: no other user can read your data. Removing a friend stops them from seeing anything of yours.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our hosting and authentication provider, on servers located in the United States. Supabase processes it on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from the Home screen.
  • Delete your account and data. Email zacharyartman@icloud.com from the address on your account and we will delete your account, workouts, and friendships within 30 days.
  • Notifications can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

Spot is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it here and update the effective date above.
